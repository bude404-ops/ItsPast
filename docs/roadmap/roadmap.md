# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Project shell, routing, CI, deployment, schema, docs, and mock-safe services.

## Phase 2 — Physical Entities
Entity CRUD through secure backend flows, map markers, and entity relationships.

## Phase 3 — Historical Data
Timeline management, source ingestion, confidence, conflict tracking.

## Phase 4 — Scanning
Camera pipeline, GPS matching, manual candidate selection.

## Phase 5 — AI Reconstruction
Evidence collection, prompt engine, provider integration, safe labeling.

## Phase 6 — Comparison
Current-vs-past sliders and historical media overlays.

## Phase 7 — AR
Historical layers aligned with physical locations.

## Phase 8 — Business
Property reports, business accounts, exports, API.

## Phase 9 — Education
Assignments, tours, and classroom history projects.

## Phase 10 — Global Physical History Graph
Structured, searchable historical identities for physical entities worldwide.
